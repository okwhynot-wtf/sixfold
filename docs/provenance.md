# Provenance and AI assistance

Sixfold is an independent project by **okwhynot-wtf**, investigating a spatial
self-similar fractal family through mathematics and visualisation.

## AI contribution

The project was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI GPT models
through ChatGPT and Codex. Assistance included exploring constructions,
developing mathematical arguments, writing verification and rendering code,
searching prior literature, and drafting documentation.

The project owner initiated and directed the investigation, assessed its
direction and visual results, and selected the public presentation and reuse
terms. The programs and certificates record the computational checks.
Independent mathematical review of the proof arguments is pending.
Specific model versions should be added where the original session records
establish them.

Useful review identifies the claim, source passage and a reproducible
calculation or mathematical objection. Repository issues can record that discussion.
