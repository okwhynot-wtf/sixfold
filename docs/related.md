# Published relatives

The prior-art search of 17 November 2026 found relevant predecessors and no
matching description of this precise family. Historical priority remains open.

| Construction | Connection to Sixfold |
| --- | --- |
| Regular Sierpiński octahedron | Six octahedrally placed copies. All six first-level pieces contain the centre. Hausdorff dimension about 2.585. |
| Syntopia and Knighty's octahedral folding constructions | A precedent for rotated octahedral fractals. A relative-rotation calculation distinguishes Sixfold's maps from the shader's single-step inverse branches. |
| Spatial Jordan arcs of Kamalutdinov, Tetenov and Vaulin | Connected spatial examples that fail the usual separation conditions, with interval topology. |
| Golden gaskets of Broomhead, Montaldi and Sidorov | An overlapping planar family with holes and computable dimensions at distinguished parameters. |
| Irrational-rotation carpets of Bandt and Mekhontsev | Connected planar examples found by computer-assisted exploration. |
| Classical Menger sponge | Spatial tunnels at every scale and Hausdorff dimension about 2.727. |

The [full comparison](../research/fractal_research_11_prior_art/PRIOR_ART.md)
contains sources, the rotation calculation, further examples and search limits.

The candidate contribution is the explicit contact-defined family, its
verified properties and two members with computed Hausdorff dimensions.
The general theories of self-similarity, dimension gaps, overlap and shrinking
loops have established predecessors.
