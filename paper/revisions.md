## Draft 0.2 — 19 November 2026

This is an editorial and proof-exposition revision. It retains the theorem conclusions and parameter scopes of draft 0.1. The archived scientific programs, certificates and data are unchanged. Independent mathematical review remains outstanding.

- Rewrote the abstract around the concrete construction and its results; added an early table separating uniform family results from the two exact dimension calculations.
- Moved the full reference algebraic-conjugate calculation to Appendix A and the inherited tilted-bore investigation to Appendix B. Appendix C maps the evidence. The shorter rational-tilt argument remains in the main text.
- Added a direct nested-connected-set proof of connectedness. Cited Badger–Vellis Theorem 1.1 as a directly inspected route to path connectedness without separation assumptions, alongside Hata.
- Made the grid-centre error plus infinite-tail bound explicit, so the finite-to-infinite step used by the packing and passage arguments is easy to identify.
- Corrected the packing statistic's description: the reported minimum is over checked same/adjacent-cell pairs, not a certified global minimum. The geometric bound for all other cells completes the separation proof; the dimension conclusion is unchanged.
- Spelled out the equal-length rotation-product argument, the topology used in the WSP definition, and the complete list of hypotheses in the Hochman application.
- Specified uniqueness of the rational-tilt root in its certified scale interval, gave the exact height-recovery formula, and clarified denominator clearing before reduction modulo 37.
- Defined winding and linking through the inclusion homomorphism into a complement's singular homology. Explained why unbounded numbers of repetitions do not prevent the shrinking concatenation from being continuous.
- Distinguished a shared periodic point from equal word maps, and the rotoreflection symmetry from a pure rotation. Marked displayed dimension decimals as approximations.
- Separated equality of map systems, bi-Lipschitz equivalence and homeomorphism in the prior-art comparison. The originality claim concerns the explicit family and its documented analysis; historical priority remains open.
- Made the compiled PDF directly available from the GitHub Pages paper page. The existing Zenodo DOI continues to cite the underlying evidence archive, which does not contain these drafts.

The 18 November six-group verification receipt is retained because the scientific inputs are unchanged. Document and website checks are refreshed for this revision in [draft-check.json](draft-check.json).
