SIXFOLD / EDITION 01

100 unique compositions: 34 landscape, 33 square, 33 portrait.
Open index.html to browse, filter and mark favourites. Download your selection as a text file, or send the S001-S100 numbers.
Favourites are stored in this browser when local storage is available; download the list to keep a portable copy.

Each aspect folder contains:
  stills: full-size JPEGs for use and selection.
  masters: lossless native PNGs of the same 100 compositions.
  videos: two H.264 MP4s at 30 fps, without audio or text.
  posters: still frames from those movies for website thumbnails.

Landscape: native 1280x720, delivered 1920x1080.
Square: native 1280x1280, delivered 1920x1920.
Portrait: native 720x1280, delivered 1080x1920.
All enlargement uses FFmpeg Lanczos. Native rendering never exceeds 1280 on either side.
Stills average four subpixel samples at native resolution; films average two. Upscaling adds no new geometric detail.

Chromatic descent: 48-second loop; copper/teal, glacier, cobalt, violet, orchid, copper/teal.
Through the passage: 36-second journey with a turn back toward the sculpture and short opening/closing fades; verdigris, moonstone, rose gold, copper/teal.
The tunnel follows phase 09's clear line through (1,0,h), in direction (-1,0,1). Two extra geometric levels improve close-wall detail.

catalogue.csv maps image IDs to files. source/manifest.json and source/palettes.json record the render recipes.
